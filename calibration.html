Withdrawal classes (added 2026-09-15)

A call retracted BEFORE its evaluation date because a load-bearing assumption was found unsupported is recorded as WITHDRAWN-THESIS-ERROR. It scores as a MISS on direction against conviction-implied probability. Detection is the process working — it is not grounds to erase the forecast.

A call retracted for reasons unrelated to the thesis is WITHDRAWN-COVERAGE-DROPPED and is EXCLUDED from the aggregate.
