The five things I'm watching (in order)

  1. Interest expense. My call says interest grows faster than renewal pricing. If quarterly interest comes in well below my model, the thesis weakens. Also watch the rate on any new bond: 6.00% on new 10-year debt fires my kill criterion (the 2035 notes trade at 5.95%).
  2. Renewal pricing (LPI). Q2 was +16.8%, below the 2026 guide of high teens to low 20s. Another print below about 17% says the slowdown is real. A bounce above 20% helps the bulls.
  3. Renewal success and term. Q1 79.1%, Q2 82.6%, term down from 61 to 54 months since Q1 2025. A third print below 85%, or term below 54 months, says customers are pushing back on price.
  4. Disposition gains. GATX booked $117.5M of the $130M legacy guide in H1. A big Q3 gain and a guide raise are likely. That lifts 2026 EPS but does not change my 2027 view: gains are cyclical, not contracted.
  5. 2026 guidance and any 2027 color. Guide is $9.90 to $10.30. H1 was $5.19. A raise is likely and is mostly gains. What matters more is anything management says about 2027 interest, refinancing, or a second year of benefit from the Wells Fargo deal.

What would change my call

  • Toward SELL: price back above about $177 with no change in the numbers; LPI below +15%; renewal success below 80% again.
  • Toward BUY: 2027 commentary that supports EPS near the Street's $11.27 (lower interest than I model, or real accretion from the Wells Fargo fleet); price below about $146.
  • Not a reason to change: an EPS beat that comes from disposition gains.
