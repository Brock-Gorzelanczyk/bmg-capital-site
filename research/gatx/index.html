Why the earnings are lower than the Street's

Only the railcars whose leases end in a given year get a new rate, about a quarter of the fleet. The Lease Price Index (the change in rate at renewal) peaked at +24.5% and printed +16.8% in Q2 2026. On the renewing slice that is worth about $45M of pretax in 2027, fading to $17M by 2029. Against that, debt went from $8.3B to $12.5B in one year, the 2026 maturities carry a 3.04% coupon and refinance near 5.3% to 5.95%, and the GABX term loan floats. Repricing cannot outrun the interest.

The buy-side lens is the third row: at 2.1x book the price needs a return on equity near 16%, and the filings show 12 to 13% with disposition gains included. The bull's best argument is the fleet mark. GATX sells its oldest cars at 1.76x book, so book understates the fleet; the model marks it at 1.04x. At a 1.14x mark the target is $177 and the call is a HOLD. That assumption is on the page because it is the one a bull should attack.
