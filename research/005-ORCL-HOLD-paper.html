WHY I AM NOT BUYING IT

  • RPO trajectory. 664 at Q1 FY27, up 209 YoY (O05 / O06 * 100). If the growth is durable and the OCI margins carry, the multiple against the RPO base is defensible.
  • Non-GAAP EPS beats persistent and large. Six of the last seven reported quarters have beaten Street consensus, with an average positive surprise in the low teens. See research/credibility/ORCL-consensus.yaml for the full ledger.
  • Real cash commitment via prepayments. 11.40 of customer prepayments received in Q1 FY27 alone — durable contract-value inflow, not just backlog.

WHAT COULD GO WRONG

  • Adjusted FCF -O10 - (O08 * 1000) - O11, not the headline O10 - O11. Sell trigger #2's FCF leg is firing on any fair reading.
  • Central return question cannot be answered from public disclosure. Whether OCI capex earns ~7-8% marginal cost of capital requires OCI segment economics Oracle does not report (M15).
  • Dilution pace. 141 shares issued via ATM in Q1 FY27 (O15 / 2878 * 100 dilution), against a stated historical rate 1 per year.
  • D&A wave. Gross PP&E 122651 up from prior-year, with construction in progress 39973 — Servers and networking equipment depreciated over 6-year estimated useful life on servers means the depreciation catch-up is booked, not deferred.
