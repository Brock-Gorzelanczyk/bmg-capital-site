BMG Capital — one-person equity research operation

A one-person operation has no supervisory analyst, no compliance review, no source-substantiation requirement, and nobody to say the argument is backwards. So those were built.

Example. A published note claimed a railcar order recovery was happening in a car type the company doesn't compete in. The company's own disclosure showed that type is 40.2% of its fleet — the largest single exposure it has. The claim was falsified. The falsified ledger caught it after an outside review pointed at it; the adversarial-review gate (Gate 4) now runs before ship so a future claim of this class fails at commit time, not after publication.
